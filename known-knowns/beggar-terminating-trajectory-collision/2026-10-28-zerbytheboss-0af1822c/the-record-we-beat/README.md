- Result: We exhibit the first explicit witness of two valid balanced initial deals in legal setting (N, R) = (16, 2) whose trajectories reach distinct playable trick states x != y such that F(x) = F(y) = z and whose common continuation from z terminates legally in 3 tricks (5 tricks total from start).
