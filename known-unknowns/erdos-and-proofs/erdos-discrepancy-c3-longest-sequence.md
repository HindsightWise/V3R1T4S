# Erdős discrepancy, C = 3: a +-1 sequence longer than 130,000 with discrepancy at most 3

✏️ **Erdős problems and proofs** · standard · tractability ★★★☆☆ · compute: laptop-hours (SAT on a 130k-variable instance; checking a candidate takes seconds) · open as of 2026-10-30

## The problem

For a +-1 sequence x_1..x_N, the discrepancy is the maximum over d >= 1 and k with kd <= N of |x_d + x_{2d} + ... + x_{kd}|. Tao (arXiv:1509.05363, 2015) proved that every infinite +-1 sequence has unbounded discrepancy, so for each C the longest length L(C) of a sequence with discrepancy <= C is finite. L(1) = 11. L(2) = 1160 (Boris Konev and Alexei Lisitsa, SAT 2014: a length-1160 sequence plus an UNSAT proof at 1161). For C = 3 only lower bounds are known. Le Bras, Gomes and Selman (arXiv:1407.2510) cite 17,000 as the record before 2014, and they proved that completely multiplicative discrepancy-3 sequences stop at exactly 127,645. Konev and Lisitsa (Artificial Intelligence 224, 2015, arXiv:1405.3097) proved the same for completely multiplicative and multiplicative sequences. They then generated an unrestricted discrepancy-3 sequence of length 130,000 whose first 127,600 terms satisfy the complete-multiplicativity conditions (files at cgi.csc.liv.ac.uk/~konev/edp), listed the unconstrained C = 3 value as ' > 130 000' in their Table 1, and remarked that their solvers struggled to extend it much further. No upper bound for unrestricted sequences is published. The AlphaEvolve survey (Georgiev, Gómez-Serrano, Tao and Wagner, arXiv:2511.02864, 2025) still cites C(3) >= 13,000, which is stale. Re-fetches of the Konev-Lisitsa page and papers on 2026-10-30 show no record above 130,000. Find a longer sequence.

## What counts as a win

Success means an explicit +-1 sequence x_1..x_N (plain text, one sign per line, 1-indexed) such that every partial sum x_d + x_{2d} + ... + x_{kd}, for every d >= 1 and every k with kd <= N, lies in [-3, 3]. Two independent checkers must confirm this. N must be STRICTLY GREATER (direction: max) than the best published length at verification time: the maximum of 130,000 (Konev-Lisitsa page https://cgi.csc.liv.ac.uk/~konev/edp and arXiv:1405.3097), any newer value on that page, and any value found by a fresh search for 'discrepancy 3' sequence records over the last 24 months. A sequence that is completely multiplicative cannot win, because it is capped at 127,645.

## Partial wins

- **p1:** A DRAT- or LRAT-checked proof (drat-trim or cake_lpr) that no discrepancy-3 sequence of a stated length N' with 130,000 < N' <= 140,000 agrees with the first 127,600 terms of Konev-Lisitsa's published length-130,000 sequence. The CNF must be generated by the claimant's own encoder and regenerated by the skeptic from a published script. After the fixed prefix is substituted, the CNF must not be refuted by unit propagation alone (the skeptic checks this), so the certificate says more than a forced-sign clash at one index. This certifies that the published prefix is a dead end at N'.

## How a win is checked

Checker A: for each d in 1..N, sum x_{kd} for k = 1..floor(N/d) and track the running maximum of |partial sum|. This is about N ln N, roughly 1.6M additions. Checker B: use independent code in another language, or a numpy cumulative sum over each strided view x[d-1::d]. Both must report a maximum <= 3 and confirm that the length is N and all entries are +-1. For p1, regenerate the CNF from the published encoder and check the proof with drat-trim. Then re-fetch the Konev-Lisitsa page and search arXiv for newer C = 3 records.

## Ideas to start from

Starting points, not instructions. The best wins came from angles nobody listed.

- Start from the published length-130,000 sequence. Keep the completely multiplicative prefix fixed up to some M < 127,645, free the tail, and grow N with incremental SAT (CaDiCaL/kissat assumptions), freeing a sliding window when it gets stuck.
- Relax complete multiplicativity only at large primes: require x_{pq} = x_p x_q for primes p, q < P and leave large primes free. This keeps most of the structure while escaping the 127,645 cap.
- Use the Konev-Lisitsa automaton encoding of partial-sum states, which needs only O(N log N) auxiliary variables, rather than cardinality constraints.
- Use local search (probSAT) seeded with the published sequence, minimising the number of violated progression sums.

## Traps

False wins others have fallen for. A claim that walks into one of these will not survive the skeptics.

- Completely multiplicative and multiplicative sequences are proven to stop at 127,645. A search confined to them cannot win.
- Every difference d and every length k counts. Checking only d up to some bound, or only full-length progressions, gives false records.
- Off-by-one: the sequence is 1-indexed (x_1 is the first term). A 0-indexed file shifts every progression.
- The discrepancy-2 and discrepancy-3 files on the Konev-Lisitsa page are different. Use the unrestricted length-130,000 file as the baseline.
