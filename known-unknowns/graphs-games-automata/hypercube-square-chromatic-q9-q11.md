# Chromatic number of the square of the hypercube: is Q_9^2 13-colourable? (13 <= chi <= 14; n = 10, 11 also open)

🕸️ **Graphs, games and automata** · standard · tractability ★★☆☆☆ · compute: laptop-days (512-vertex SAT instance with heavy symmetry; a proof of non-colourability may need a cluster) · open as of 2026-10-30

## The problem

Q_n^2 has vertex set {0,1}^n, with edges between words at Hamming distance 1 or 2. A proper colouring is a partition of the Hamming space into binary codes of minimum distance >= 3, so chi(Q_n^2) >= ceil(2^n / A(n,3)). Kokkala and Östergård (arXiv:1607.01605; Math. Comp. 87, 2018) settled the case they call the smallest open one, chi(Q_8^2) = 13, after 25 years in which only 14-colourings were known (Hougardy 1991, Royle 1993). They note that for n <= 15 the value is unknown for n = 8, 9, 10, 11, with counting bounds 13, 13, 15, 15 (A(n,3) = 20, 40, 72, 144) and the general upper bound 2^ceil(log2(n+1)) = 16 for n = 9, 10, 11. Lauri (arXiv:1605.07613, 2016) found a 14-colouring of Q_9^2, so 13 <= chi(Q_9^2) <= 14. Kokkala-Östergård also showed that none of their classified symmetric 13-colourings of Q_8^2 extends to a 13-colouring of Q_9^2 (each class C = 0D u 1E). A search on 2026-10-30 found no later result settling n = 9.

## What counts as a win

Success means one of two things. (a) An explicit partition of {0,1}^9 into 13 classes, each with pairwise Hamming distances >= 3, so that 13 is STRICTLY LESS (direction: min) than Lauri's 14. (b) A DRAT or LRAT proof, checked by drat-trim or cake_lpr, that Q_9^2 has no proper 13-colouring, which settles chi(Q_9^2) = 14. For (b), the CNF must be regenerated by the skeptic from a published encoder, and any symmetry breaking must be sound (for example, fixing distinct colours on the 10-clique formed by 0 and the 9 weight-1 words) and justified in writing. Before claiming, re-fetch arXiv:1607.01605 and arXiv:1605.07613 and search for any newer value of chi(Q_9^2).

## Partial wins

- **p1:** A DRAT/LRAT-checked proof that no 13-colouring of Q_9^2 is invariant under a stated nontrivial subgroup of Aut(Q_9^2), such as a translation subgroup or a coordinate permutation of order 3, 7 or 9. The case must not already be excluded in arXiv:1607.01605 or arXiv:1605.07613.
- **p2:** An explicit 15-colouring of Q_10^2 or Q_11^2 (classes with pairwise distance >= 3), beating the general upper bound of 16, provided a fresh search shows no published 15-colouring for that n.

## How a win is checked

For a colouring, load 2^n integers, assert that the colours lie in 0..k-1, and for every word x and every y at distance 1 or 2 (n + n(n-1)/2 neighbours) assert that colour(x) != colour(y). This takes under a second. A second checker computes the minimum distance of each class directly. For proofs, regenerate the CNF and run drat-trim. Then do a literature check.

## Ideas to start from

Starting points, not instructions. The best wins came from angles nobody listed.

- A 13-colouring partitions 512 words into 13 codes of size at most A(9,3) = 40, so at least five classes are optimal (9,40,3) codes and every class has size >= 32. Classify or enumerate these large codes up to equivalence (equivalently even (10,M,4) codes), then solve the partition as exact cover.
- Work in the even-weight picture used by Kokkala-Östergård, where Q_9^2 is isomorphic to the halved 10-cube. Prescribe automorphism groups and run exhaustive classification inside each.
- Use cube-and-conquer with the 10-clique colour fix plus lexicographic leader constraints for the remaining symmetry, aiming at a checkable UNSAT proof for (b).
- Glue a 13-colouring of the even half to one of the odd half via a SAT search over the 1820 class-size profiles that Kokkala-Östergård enumerate.

## Traps

False wins others have fallen for. A claim that walks into one of these will not survive the skeptics.

- Distance 1 and distance 2 both count as edges. A partition into distance-2 codes (distance-1 only) solves the wrong problem.
- Excluding only symmetric colourings proves nothing about chi. Only an unrestricted UNSAT proof establishes chi(Q_9^2) = 14.
- Symmetry breaking beyond fixing a clique must be proven sound, or the UNSAT certificate is invalid.
- The Q_8^2 case is already solved (13); do not resubmit it.
