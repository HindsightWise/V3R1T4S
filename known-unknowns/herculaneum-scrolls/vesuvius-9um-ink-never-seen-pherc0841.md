## The problem

Most still-unread prize volumes only have native 8.6-9.4 µm scans (the Reader v2 README counts 20 of 22 First Letters scrolls without a 2.4 µm scan). That makes the team's 9 µm surface-render ink models the working tool for First Letters searches. Cross-scroll generalization is a named bottleneck on scrollprize.org/2026_open_problems (updated 2026-10-14): 'Ink models may work on one scroll but not another'. The released models (huggingface.co/scrollprize/ink_9um, MIT, 14 checkpoints = 2 seeds x 7 steps, 2026-08-14) were trained on aligned labels from PHerc. 0139, 1667, Paris 4 and 0814 plus 5 native 0139 segments. They ship no metrics, and the model card calls them 'far from optimized'. TEST SET (S3 listings fetched 2026-10-29, HTTP 200): PHerc0841 is on neither the Grand Prize list nor the First Letters list. It has a 9.366 µm / 113 keV scan (volumes/20250821151531-9.366um-1.2m-113keV-masked.zarr) and a 2.403 µm / 77 keV scan (20260319124803). Its three official segments are 20260220213127-w00, 20260220214732-auto_grown_20260220144552896 ('ag144') and 20260221022814-auto_grown_20260220174252405 ('ag174'). ## What counts as a win

PROTOCOL '0841-9um' (pinned). score_0841.py is written by the scout on day 1 and frozen by sha256 in a hash-locked METRIC.sha256 before any model is scored. Input: one ink map per segment on the 9.366 µm canvas, computed only from the official surface-volumes/9.366um-1.2m-113keV-volume-20250821151531.zarr; depth window, layer order and flip-TTA are declared in the attempt manifest. Mapping: label-canvas pixels go to the map canvas by the ratio of the two published tifxyz grid scales (meta.json 'scale'), with bilinear sampling. One integer offset per segment is fixed before any scoring by phase correlation of the two official surface volumes' middle layers, never by a model score. Metrics: (1) ROC AUC (ties averaged) of the map against inklabels > 0 over pixels with supervision > 0 (primary); (2) the same AUC over validation > 0 pixels (robustness); (3) FA80 = fraction of label-0 supervised pixels at or above the threshold that recalls 80% of ink pixels, averaged over the three segments. GATE A (baselines). In a hash-locked BASELINES.json the scout scores, under this exact protocol and in both layer orders (each model keeps its better order per segment, which favours the baselines): Reader V3 (domenicor046/reader-v3 @ aee1d64), Reader v2 step-040000, Hecate 9.6 µm (renders resampled to 9.6 µm as its card requires), v8-in (YoussefMoNader/ink-8um-v8in), all 14 ink_9um checkpoints, ink9um-dense w016excluded step075000, Nieuwlaar dense-native, TRACE sA, sB, v3A and v3B (Jashann/vesuvius-scrolling release v1.0, sha256 as in its MODEL_CARD), A-hn seeds 0-2 (Prodigle/pherc0306b-ink-ahn) and any other public single-network 9 µm ink checkpoint the scout finds on the day BASELINES.json is frozen. Definitions: B_s = best baseline AUC on segment s; B_mean = best baseline 3-segment mean; F_min = lowest baseline FA80; V_s = best baseline validation-region AUC. GATE B (leakage). leak_audit.py over the training manifest and the whole initialisation and teacher chain must find: no PHerc0841 path or hash (CT, renders, meshes, labels, team ink maps, predictions, pseudo-labels); no initialisation from Hecate or ink_canonical_2um (their documented supervision is the ink bucket that holds 841/) or from anything fine-tuned from them, from any TRACE checkpoint (selected on PHerc0841 labels), or from any other network whose training read PHerc0841 bytes or whose checkpoint, layer order or depth window was chosen on PHerc0841 scores; networks trained only on other scrolls' renders and team 2.4 µm maps (ink9um-dense, Reader v2, Reader V3) are allowed starts; teacher outputs used only on other scrolls. GATE C (selection). One network, no ensemble of separately trained networks. Its checkpoint sha256, layer order and TTA are declared before the first PHerc0841 inference. Every PHerc0841 scoring is appended to EVALS_0841.log, and the count is reported. GATE D (nulls). The same weights, with the declared TTA, on the reversed layer order and on a seeded z-shuffled layer order give AUC <= 0.65 on every segment (a network trained on, or averaged over, both layer orders therefore fails this gate). SUCCESS requires all of: mean AUC >= B_mean + 0.03; AUC >= B_s on at least two of the three segments and >= B_s - 0.01 on the third; FA80 <= F_min; validation-region AUC >= V_s - 0.01 on every segment. This is an internal benchmark. It is credited officially only if scrollprize.org/winners credits a Progress Prize submission made by the human.

## Partial wins

- **p1:** Camera truth at 116 keV. A single network obeying Gates B-C, extended to PHerc0343 and PHerc0343P: no CT, render, label or map bytes of either, and no initialisation from Reader v2, Reader V3 or any other weights whose training names PHerc0343P. Run on the official 8.64 µm / 116 keV surface volume of PHerc0343P segment 20250511003658 (3440 x 2060 map, declared layer order), it must score AUC >= 0.83 against the camera masks of DomRusso2/ir-truth-9um @ 7e96b96 (data/truth_ink.png against data/truth_noink.png inside data/valid.png; sha256 recorded by the scout), with the 2000-random-placement control at p <= 0.001. The skeptic recomputes this with a numpy re-implementation of scripts/score_map.py. For context: the best fragment-naive model, v8-in, scores 0.744 and the team's 2.2 µm map 0.909.
- **p2:** Leave-1667-out at a frozen threshold. A single network trained with zero PHerc1667 bytes: the ink_9um labels minus the six 1667 representations, plus any non-1667 data, with initialisation only from weights whose documented training excludes PHerc1667 (every released ink_9um, Reader and Hecate checkpoint saw 1667). Its 8-bit maps on the aligned 1667 segments are thresholded at the lowest exact maximiser of pooled F1 on pherc1667-w013 (integers 0-255, positive = score >= threshold, unique-pixel counting as in khj1222 docs/26). Mean F1 over pherc1667-w023, w028, w029 and w031 must be >= 0.5806 (= the docs/26 LOSO base 0.5306 + 0.05). Gate: the scout first reproduces the docs/26 base 0.5306 within 0.005 with the same scorer, from khj1222's archived predictions or a retrained LOSO arm.

## How a win is checked

After the human has accepted the data terms, the skeptic fetches the three segments' 20260918 label zarrs (about 1 MB) and checks their sha256 against the scout's record. The skeptic then: (1) checks METRIC.sha256 and BASELINES.json; (2) recomputes AUC, validation AUC and FA80 for the frozen claimed maps and for two baselines' stored maps, requiring |difference| < 1e-6; (3) re-runs the claimed checkpoint on one seeded segment at the declared settings (minutes on a consumer GPU, about an hour on CPU or MPS) and requires Pearson r > 0.99 with the frozen map; (4) recomputes the reversed and z-shuffled nulls on that segment; (5) runs leak_audit.py over the training manifest and initialisation chain; (6) before any claim, re-reads the S3 PHerc0841 listings, the HF ink bucket, scrollprize.org/prizes and /winners, khj1222 docs/32, the Reader V3 repo, the Jashann/vesuvius-scrolling releases and the Hugging Face ink-model listings for newer models. For p1 the skeptic recomputes the AUC and the random-placement p from the frozen map. For p2 the skeptic recomputes the w013 threshold and the four F1 values from the frozen 8-bit maps.

## Ideas to start from

Starting points, not instructions. The best wins came from angles nobody listed.

- Start from Reader V3, whose train_config names no PHerc0841 data, or from Reader v2 (both allowed by Gate B; TRACE checkpoints are not). Train further with larger tiles and stronger 113 keV augmentation in one declared layer order: training on both orders, or averaging both at inference, fails Gate D. p1 needs a start free of 0343P, such as ink_9um or v8-in.
- Distil an ensemble into one network on non-0841 renders: Reader V3, v8-in and ink_9um checkpoint averages. In the KLAVIS suite, the mean of v2 and Hecate beats either model alone.
- Build dense native-scan labels from the team's 2.4 µm maps on objects scanned at both resolutions (Reader v2 used six), and add them to the ink_9um corpus. Add scan-protocol randomisation: blur, contrast, decohesion and noise.
- Fix a label-free layer-order rule on non-0841 data before scoring, for example taking the order whose output is more spread out, as reported for ink_9um on the project Discord.
- For p2, follow the docs/26 calibration rule exactly and report seeds separately.

## Traps

False wins others have fallen for. A claim that walks into one of these will not survive the skeptics.

- Never compare with the published AUCs: two harnesses on the native 9.366 µm renders disagree by about 0.04 on the same map (Reader V3 on w00: 0.881 in its own suite, 0.916 in khj1222 docs/32), and khj1222 docs/33 and docs/35 (2026-10-29/30) report Reader V3 at 0.930 / 0.859 / 0.862 and ink_9um seed43 at 0.891 / 0.800 / 0.820 on level-2 renders of the 2.403 µm scan, which is not this protocol's input. Only BASELINES.json under the frozen script counts.
- The 2.4 µm and 9 µm canvases are not a pure rescale: DomRusso2 and the khj1222 notes on villa #2243 report an extra 6-8 px shift. Fix the offset once from CT-to-CT alignment, never per model.
- Reversed layer order collapses AUC to about 0.54-0.62 for every model. Declare the order rule before any 0841 inference.
- Hecate and ink_canonical_2um were trained on the ink bucket that holds 841/, the TRACE checkpoints were selected on PHerc0841, and Reader v2, Reader V3 and TRACE v3A/v3B were trained on PHerc0343P. Using Hecate, ink_canonical_2um or TRACE as initialisation voids the main claim; using Reader v2, Reader V3 or TRACE v3 voids p1.
- Choosing checkpoints, thresholds, directions or depth windows by 0841 scores is test-set selection. EVALS_0841.log is reported with every claim.
- The human labels were drawn on CT renders and the team's 2.4 µm maps, so AUC measures agreement with annotators. That is why p1 adds a camera truth.
- Label smoothing 0.5 puts outputs at about 0.25-0.75. AUC is unaffected, but FA80 thresholds must be recomputed for each model.
- Data terms: a human accepts the Vesuvius Challenge terms (CC BY-NC 4.0; dl.ash2txt.org/LICENSE.txt) before any download. Agents never accept terms, never redistribute labels or renders, and never post ink images of unread scrolls.
